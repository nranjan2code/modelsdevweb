# Public-market context

Model Pulse tracks the organizations around model economics without becoming a trading product.
The current public feature is a curated registry of model labs, cloud and inference providers,
compute suppliers, data-center operators, IT services firms and selected logistics proxies.

## What we publish

- company identity, role and direct/indirect relationship to the model economy;
- ticker, exchange, country, currency and primary/secondary listing structure;
- exchange-local trading sessions, including split sessions and weekends;
- public or private status;
- explicit exposure notes explaining why each entity is included.

One company is one economic entity. An ADR and its local ordinary share are listings of the same
company and do not create two entries in the registry.

## What we do not publish

The free-only policy excludes third-party stock quotes, OHLC history, returns, market-cap baskets,
IPO monitoring and quote downloads. Not trading does not change the licensing question: putting a
price on a public webpage is still public display. Tavily and SEC EDGAR are not used as stock-price
sources.

This boundary keeps the page factual and useful without presenting a free personal-use market-data
feed as a public redistribution license. Current prices remain available from the linked exchange,
company or finance source.

## Editorial rule

Public-company exposure is context, not a model or provider ranking. A cloud partnership, strategic
investment, compute supply relationship, implementation contract and logistics proxy are different
facts and are labelled separately. A private lab never receives a synthetic ticker.
